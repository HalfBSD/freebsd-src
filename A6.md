### A6. Historical non-HDA audio-controller backends

**Purpose and locations:** Review legacy PCM hardware implementations under `sys/dev/sound/pci` and corresponding module selections under `sys/modules/sound/driver`. Initial candidates include older AC’97-era and pre-HDA controllers such as `ich`, `via82c686`, `via8233`, `neomagic`, `solo`, `t4dwave`, and `vibes`. The existing audit identified these as historical controller backends while explicitly retaining the common PCM framework, mixers, HDA, USB audio, and virtual audio. HALFBSD-ARCHITECTURAL-AUDIT

**HalfBSD policy:** HalfBSD should support modern workstation audio paths, especially onboard motherboard audio, GPU HDMI/DisplayPort audio, USB audio, and ordinary software playback from optical media. A driver is not removable merely because it is old; it should be removed only when the underlying controller family is outside HalfBSD’s supported modern-amd64 hardware baseline.

**Pre-removal audit requirement:** Before modifying the source tree, enumerate every supported PCI/device ID for each proposed driver and map those IDs to actual controller and chipset families. For every candidate, report:

- supported hardware families and PCI IDs;
- approximate hardware generations;
- whether the controller is AC’97, HDA, or another architecture;
- newest hardware supported by that driver;
- whether that hardware can plausibly appear in a modern amd64 desktop or laptop;
- bus type and attachment paths;
- shared sound-framework dependencies;
- reverse dependencies from retained drivers or utilities;
- exact capability lost if the driver is removed.

Present the proposed removal list for review before deleting any implementation.

**Required retained functionality:** Do **not** remove or weaken:

- the common sound/PCM framework;
- `snd_hda` and modern HDA controller/codec support;
- onboard analog motherboard audio using HDA codecs;
- headphone, microphone, line-input, and mixer functionality provided through retained HDA hardware;
- AMD/NVIDIA/Intel HDMI or DisplayPort audio paths;
- USB Audio Class support;
- virtual audio facilities;
- shared mixer infrastructure;
- audio support needed by browsers, Wayland applications, media players, or normal workstation software.

**Optical-media boundary:** Legacy sound-controller removal must not affect CD, DVD, or Blu-ray data access or normal digital media playback. Preserve all storage and device infrastructure required for:

- USB optical drives;
- SATA/PATA optical drives where otherwise supported;
- CAM/SCSI optical-device handling;
- reading audio CDs digitally;
- DVD/Blu-ray data access;
- software decoding and playback through retained HDA, HDMI/DisplayPort, or USB audio.

Do not treat historical analog CD-ROM audio cabling or motherboard CD-IN headers as a reason to retain an obsolete sound-controller family unless the controller itself otherwise falls within HalfBSD’s supported hardware policy.

**Dependencies:** Shared PCM, mixer, codec, PCI, DMA, interrupt, firmware, HDA, USB-audio, and virtual-audio infrastructure must remain unless independently proven unused by all retained hardware. Remove only controller-specific source, module/build entries, device IDs, firmware, documentation, tests, and installed-file integration belonging exclusively to hardware approved for retirement.

**Disposition and impact:** Remove only controller families whose supported hardware is conclusively obsolete under the HalfBSD hardware baseline. Do not use driver age, source-file age, or AC’97 association alone as sufficient justification. Modern onboard motherboard audio and all retained digital audio paths must continue to function.

**Benefit / risk / confidence:** Moderate reduction in historical driver code and probe/module surface. Low architectural benefit compared with networking/storage cuts, but potentially worthwhile once the hardware boundary is proven. Moderate hardware-compatibility risk; confidence should be assigned per driver rather than to the category as a whole.

**Validation:** After removal, test at minimum:

- onboard analog speaker output;
- headphone output;
- microphone/line input where available;
- mixer volume and mute;
- AMD/Intel/NVIDIA HDMI or DisplayPort audio where applicable;
- USB audio playback and recording;
- browser/media-player audio;
- Wayland desktop audio;
- suspend/resume;
- USB Blu-ray/DVD/CD drive detection and playback;
- SATA optical-drive detection and playback where supported.
